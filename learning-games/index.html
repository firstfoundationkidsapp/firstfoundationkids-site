Learning games

Learning games for kids ages 2 to 7

34 playful worlds with Pip the frog: counting, letters, reading, handwriting, telling time, science, shapes, feelings, music, fruits and vegetables, and first mouse and keyboard skills. Every world has 30 levels for each of three age groups, and Pip reads every question out loud, so children can play before they can read.

Made for three age groups

Tadpoles · Ages 2–3

Gentle start: few choices, small numbers, big targets.

Froglets · Ages 4–5

More choices, bigger numbers, lowercase letters.

Jumpers · Ages 6–7

The trickiest rounds: adding, mixing, longer patterns.
